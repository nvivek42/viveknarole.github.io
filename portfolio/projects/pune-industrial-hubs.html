Pune Industrial Hubs Analysis

May 2025 4 weeks GitHub Live Demo
Primary Research Systems Mapping Markdown Static Site

The Problem

Pune is marketed as "Oxford of the East" and "Detroit of India," but actionable intelligence for career decisions is scattered across job boards, outdated blogs, recruiter pitches, and word of mouth. No single source maps: which hubs actually have which companies, what the commute/rent trade-offs are, where specific roles (embedded, backend, ML, hardware) concentrate, and what the constraints are (water, power, traffic, flooding, transit).

People make 5–10 year location decisions on vibes and LinkedIn posts. That's expensive.

Approach

Primary research compiled from multiple sources, cross-verified, and structured as a decision support system:

  • Hub identification: Mapped 8 distinct industrial corridors (Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Magarpatta, Baner-Balewadi, Shivajinagar, Bhosari, KP-Kalyani Nagar, Chakan-Talegaon)
  • Company census: Cataloged 50+ companies per hub with sector, size, and role-type tags (product vs. services, embedded vs. cloud, etc.)
  • Constraint matrix: 12 constraint categories per hub — commute time, rent range, flood risk, water security, power reliability, metro connectivity, social infrastructure, noise/pollution, talent density, visa/sponsorship friendliness, cost of living index, future development pipeline
  • Geographic clustering: Visualized hub adjacency and overlap zones (e.g., Baner-Balewadi ↔ Hinjewadi talent flow)
  • Decision matrix: Weighted scoring framework where users input priorities (commute <30min, rent <₹25k, product companies, metro access) and get ranked hub recommendations
Source transparency: Every company listing cites source (company website, LinkedIn, news, government filings). Constraints cite municipal data, resident forums, and on-ground verification where possible.

Solution

A structured Markdown knowledge base (in raw/ and analysis/) rendered as static HTML with:

  • 8 hub profiles — companies, constraints, verdict
  • Cross-hub comparison table (12 constraints × 8 hubs)
  • Role-type index — which hubs for embedded, backend, ML, hardware, devops, frontend
  • Decision worksheet — plug in your weights, get ranked output
  • Source register — every claim traced to a document/URL/date

Published as static pages (no JS required for core content) with dark mode, print-friendly CSS, and llms.txt for AI ingestion.

Impact & Metrics

8 Hubs Mapped
50+ Companies Cataloged
12 Constraint Categories
4 Decision Scenarios

Used by 3 peers for job relocation decisions. One avoided a 2-hour daily commute by choosing Kharadi over Hinjewadi based on the constraint matrix. The decision worksheet reduces "where should I live?" from weeks of research to a 20-minute structured exercise.

What Went Wrong

  • Manual data, no refresh automation. Census is from May 2025 with no scheduled updates. A scheduled scraper for company career pages + LinkedIn API would keep listings current.
  • Stale rental data. Rent ranges are broad point-in-time estimates. Integration with MagicBricks/99acres API for real-time price bands per hub would make it decision-usable longer.
  • Static commute estimates. Peak vs. off-peak transit times differ by 2–3×. Integration with Google Maps / Ola Maps API for dynamic routing would reflect reality.
  • Municipal data only for utilities. Water/power/flood reliability sourced from reports, not resident experience. Crowdsourced constraint reporting would be more trustworthy.
  • Single-city scope. Framework is portable (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, NCR) but not yet applied elsewhere.
