Quick decision framework: Identify your cluster → Check salary band → Pick live/work zone → Factor commute tolerance. This page gives you the cluster map; you supply the constraints.

Five Economic Clusters (Top-Down)

Pune's economy organises around five clusters. Each has distinct salary bands, growth vectors, and geographic anchors.

Auto & Manufacturing

Automotive & Industrial

₹8–25L · Median ₹14L (3–6 yr)

57 auto GCCs (44.5% of India's), 65 mfg centres. Anchors: Cummins, Tata Motors, Bajaj, Mercedes-Benz, VW, JCB. Tier-1 supply chain dense.

📍 Chakan, Talegaon, Bhosari, Ranjangaon
BFSI & GCC

Banking, Financial Services & Insurance

₹10–30L · Median ₹18L (3–6 yr)

60+ GCCs. Barclays, Citi, Deutsche, HSBC, BNY, Fiserv, Mastercard, ZS. Heavy .NET/Java, data, risk, quant. Highest Gen-3/4 density.

📍 Kharadi, Yerawada, Viman Nagar, Mundhwa
Tech & SaaS

Technology / SaaS / Product

₹12–35L · Median ₹20L (3–6 yr)

74 centres — largest single category. Persistent, Zensar, KPIT, Mphasis, TCS/Infosys/Wipro product arms, pure-play SaaS. Cloud, AI/ML, devtools.

📍 Hinjewadi Ph 1–3, Balewadi, Baner, Aundh
Embedded & R&D

Embedded, Semiconductor, Deep Tech

₹10–28L · Median ₹16L (3–6 yr)

NVIDIA, Lattice, Qualcomm (via partners), KPIT, Tata Elxsi, KPIT, KPIT. ECU, firmware, VLSI, automotive software. Niche, sticky, high retention.

📍 Hinjewadi, Shivajinagar (ICC), Talawade
Services & Support

IT Services / BPM / Support

₹4–12L · Median ₹7L (0–3 yr)

High volume, lower ceiling. TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture delivery centres. Entry gateway, internal mobility to product/GCC.

📍 Hinjewadi Ph 1, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Hadapsar

Cluster Flow: Where People Move

Services/BPM (Hinjewadi/Magarpatta) ↓ 1–2 yr skill-up, internal referral Tech/SaaS Product (Hinjewadi/Baner) ←──┐ ↓ specialization, cloud/AI │ Embedded/R&D (Hinjewadi/Shivajinagar) │ ↓ domain depth │ BFSI/GCC Quant/Data/Risk (Kharadi) ─────┤ (cross-cluster moves common at 3–5 yr) ↓ │ Auto/Mfg Digital Twin/IIoT (Chakan/Talawade) (niche, high retention)

Typical progression: Services → Product → Specialized R&D/GCC. Cross-cluster jumps happen at 3–5 yr via referral or upskilling. Auto/Mfg digital roles increasingly recruit from Tech/Embedded.

Growth Vectors (Next 3–5 Years)

  • EV & Battery: Chakan/Talegaon expanding — Tata, Bajaj, VW, new cell plants. Mechanical→Electrical→Software shift.
  • AI/ML in BFSI: Kharadi GCCs building fraud, credit, quant models in-house. Python/ML talent premium.
  • Semiconductor/EDA: NVIDIA, Lattice, Qualcomm ecosystem deepening in Shivajinagar/Hinjewadi. VLSI, firmware, compiler roles.
  • Defence/Aerospace: Lohegaon, Chakan corridors — DRDO, Tata, Airbus, Lockheed suppliers. Embedded+safety-critical.
  • Data Centres: Talawade, Pirangut, Ranjangaon — hyperscaler land banking. Infra, networking, ops roles.

Decision Checklist for Newcomers

  1. Pick cluster first — aligns with your stack (Java/.NET→BFSI, Embedded→Auto/R&D, Cloud/Fullstack→Tech/SaaS).
  2. Set commute budget — <30 min ideal, 45 max. Metro changes Hinjewadi↔Central math.
  3. Match rent to salary band — don't overspend on Koregaon Park if in Services cluster.
  4. Verify shuttle/metro — most GCCs/IT parks run shuttles; factor door-to-door, not station-to-station.
  5. Plan 2-yr move — start in Services/BPM if needed, target Product/GCC/Embedded jump at 18–24 mo.
