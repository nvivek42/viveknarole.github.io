# Portfolio

This portfolio exists to capture what is actually worth keeping: real work, real learning, and real tradeoffs.

It is intentionally structured to support both human discovery and machine-readability through stable categories, clear tags, and searchable summaries.

## Why this exists

The portfolio is not a generic personal homepage. It is a curated record of:

- work shaped by ambiguity and system-level thinking
- projects that reflect actual constraints and tradeoffs
- lessons that were learned under pressure, not after the fact

## BMAD and verification principles

Entries are written with an explicit standard:

- Bold Claim: what is the core point?
- Pattern Observed: what pattern did I notice?
- Action Taken: what did I do?
- Result: what changed?
- Lesson: what is worth carrying forward?
- Verification: how do I know it is true?

## Searchability and discovery

This portfolio is designed so it is easy to find and easy to trust.

- clear titles and dates
- descriptive tags
- plain-language summaries
- stable category structure
- direct links between source content and public pages
